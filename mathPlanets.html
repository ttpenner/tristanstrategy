The 5 Planets and Their Sun

Important: The diagram is NOT drawn to scale

  1. Find the relative distances of each planet to the sun, relative to planet 1 using the provided solar values. Assume that the universe is 3d.
  2. Find the absolute distances of each planet to the sun, to the nearest kilometer. You will need to use trigonometry to solve this one.
  3. Using your answers from part 2, find the distance from the sun to the solar system edge, or in other words the size of the solar system.
